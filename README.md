## About

My work sits between mathematics and imaging: segmentation, radiomics, and building pipelines that hold up on real clinical data across different hospitals and machines. I am interested in representation learning and in whether a model stays robust and points at the right thing, rather than in benchmark position alone.

## Research

**Quantitative ultrasound imaging** — TU Dresden, Jan 2026 to present
Developing the B-mode radiomics pipeline of the open-source QuantUS platform for first-order and GLCM texture feature extraction, and studying how features from standard B-mode images relate to those computed directly from raw RF signals, with phantom-based normalization for device-independent results.

**Multimodal oncology imaging** — IIT Jammu, Jun 2026 to present
Built a unified pipeline for the MICCAI 2026 HECKTOR challenge (head-and-neck PET/CT, 782 patients, 8 hospitals) covering tumor and lymph-node segmentation, TN staging, and recurrence-free survival prediction, containerized with Docker for offline evaluation.

**Semantic segmentation of novel objects** — IISER Thiruvananthapuram, Aug 2025 to Dec 2025
Explored segmentation of both known and novel objects in driving scenes by combining segmentation models with lightweight vision-language models, and evaluated behavior on in-distribution and out-of-distribution data.

**Statistical learning** — IIT Madras, May 2025 to Jul 2025
Implemented classical and Bayesian machine learning models from scratch in NumPy and pandas, with applied work in scikit-learn and PyMC, and compared their performance on public datasets.

## Projects

**Deep reinforcement learning for low-altitude UAV navigation**
Custom 3D Gymnasium environment with simplified flight physics, LiDAR-style ray sensing, and procedurally generated terrain. Trained a PPO agent with curriculum learning for evasive navigation to target waypoints, with interactive visualization in Pygame and Ursina.
